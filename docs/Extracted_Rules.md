# Document Verification Rules

These rules have been extracted and translated into plain English from the original `Rules.docx` notes. They are used to validate the official documents provided by different departments for onboarding.

## 1. Authority Letter
*   **Letterhead:** Must be printed on the official department letterhead.
*   **Focal Person:** Must clearly state the name and designation of the focal person.
*   **Authentication:** Must be duly signed and officially stamped by the department.

## 2. Account Maintenance Certificate
*   **Formatting:** The format of the certificate must be flawless and standard.
*   **Authentication:** Must be duly signed and stamped by the issuing bank.
*   **Consistency:** The bank details (Account Title, Account Number, and Branch Number/Name) must perfectly match the details provided in both the Tripartite and Bilateral agreements.

## 3. Application Form (In-Direct Customer)
*   **Organization Details:** Organization Name must be filled in. If the organization has an NTN or Registration Number, it must be provided. Country of incorporation must be stated.
*   **Account Information:** The Title of Account and the Bank Account Number must exactly match what is written on the Account Maintenance Certificate.
*   **Business Classification:** The "Nature of Business", "Organization Type", and "Business Classification" checkboxes must be accurately ticked. If none apply, it should be specified under "Others".
*   **Licenses:** If there are any relevant licenses, they must be mentioned.
*   **Personnel Details:** The details of the Proprietor, Directors, or Partners (including names and CNICs) must be completely filled out; this is mandatory.
*   **Authentication:** **Every single page** must be signed and stamped.
*   **Declarations:** 
    *   The "Declaration of Sub-Member" section must be signed and stamped.
    *   The "Aggregator" section (which belongs to the KPITB) must also be properly signed and stamped.

## 4. Tripartite Agreement
*   **Parties Involved:** Generally involves three parties: 1-Link (Biller), KPITB (Bill Aggregator), and the Sub-biller (the onboarding organization/department).
*   **Organization Name Check:** Ensure the exact name of the onboarding department is written correctly in the preamble/paragraphs (watch out for placeholder copy-paste errors like "Government Degree College" when it should be "Directorate General of Archaeology").
*   **Bank Details Verification (Crucial):** Typically under bullet point 2, sub-point 5 (note that numbering might sometimes be inconsistent like 3, 1, 2, 3), the Bank details (Account Title and Branch) must exactly match the Account Maintenance Certificate. 
*   **Signatures:** Must contain the signatures of 1-Link, the Sub-biller, the Bill Aggregator, and the respective witnesses for each party.

## 5. Bilateral Agreement (Service Level Agreement)
*   **Platform Mention:** The document must mention the correct digital platform, e.g., "Digital Muhasil" or "PayMin".
*   **Organization Name:** The specific department or organization name must be clearly stated.
*   **Text Integrity:** The main body text (A, B, C, D points, etc.) must remain intact and unmodified from the standard template.
*   **Dates & Authorized Persons:** The effective date and authorized person details should be checked (though the date is sometimes intentionally left blank).
*   **Section 5 (Transaction Charges):** Section 5.2 must explicitly mention transaction charges (e.g., "as per prevailing charges of 1-Link").
*   **Section 6 (Account Numbers):** Must list the relevant bank account numbers. The account number here must exactly match the Account Maintenance Certificate. 
*   **Authentication & Witnesses:** Must be signed and stamped everywhere required. Ensure Party A and Party B witnesses are present. If Party B witnesses are missing, another director or representative from the department might have signed as a witness; this needs to be verified.

## 6. E-Stamp Papers (Participation Memorandum & SLA)
*   **Visual Authentication:** The print should visually resemble an original E-stamp paper (typically a brownish color texture), not just a plain A4 white paper printout.
*   **Text Validation:** Check the Non-judicial description, First Party, and Second Party details on the stamp paper to ensure they are accurate and match the intended agreement.
*   **Watermark:** Ensure the E-stamp watermark and extensions are valid.

## 7. Business Requirement Document (BRD)
*   **Content:** Must list the services the department provides that generate revenue (e.g., filming fees, photography fees, ticket entries) and confirm that these payments are required to be digitized.

## 8. Formal Request Letter for Onboarding
*   **Subject Line:** The subject must clearly state the intent, for example: "Onboarding as a sub-biller with KPITB".
*   **Content:** The letter must explicitly request that the KPITB onboard them as a sub-biller.

## 9. General Requirements
*   **CNIC Copies:** Copies of the CNIC for the authorized persons must be attached and verified.
