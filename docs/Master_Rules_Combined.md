# Financial Document Verification System – Master Business Rules

This document consolidates all business rules, checks, and formatting requirements for verifying onboarding documents submitted by sub-billers to the KPITB (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Information Technology Board). No details have been omitted.

---

## 1. Required Documents
The system shall verify the presence of the required document set:
1. Tripartite Agreement
2. Bilateral Agreement
3. Account Maintenance Certificate
4. 1-Link Application Form (In-Direct Customer)
5. Authority Letter
6. Schedule of Charges Agreement
7. Business Requirement Document (BRD)
8. Formal Request Letter
9. Declaration of Sub-Biller / Supporting Documents where applicable (e.g., CNIC copies, E-Stamp Papers)

*Note: The ninth checklist point is excluded from the current verification scope where specified, but CNIC copies and E-Stamps are checked when present.*

---

## 2. Authority Letter
**Content Requirements**
The system shall verify that the Authority Letter:
- Is prepared on the organization's official department letterhead.
- Contains the focal person's name.
- Contains the focal person's designation.
- Contains the required authority/organization information.
- Contains all required information in the prescribed format.
- **Account maintenance details must appear at the top** of the authority letter.

**Signature & Stamp Requirements**
- The Authority Letter **must be duly signed** by the authorized head/authority.
- The Authority Letter **must contain the required official stamp** where prescribed.
- The system shall verify the presence and readability of the required stamp.

---

## 3. Account Maintenance Certificate
**Content Requirements**
The system shall verify that the Account Maintenance Certificate:
- Is issued on the bank's official letterhead (color is not a requirement, but it must be official).
- Follows the required bank certificate format flawlessly.
- Contains the required account-holder information.
- Contains the bank account number.
- Contains the IBAN where required.
- Contains the required bank information.
- Contains complete and readable information.
- **Consistency Check:** The Account Title, Account Number, and Branch Name/Number must perfectly match the details provided in both the Tripartite and Bilateral agreements.

**Signature & Stamp Requirements**
- The Certificate **must be duly signed** by the authorized bank representative.
- The Certificate **must contain the required bank stamp**.
- The system shall verify stamp presence, location, readability, and required information. Stamp color is not considered a validity requirement.

---

## 4. 1-Link Application Form (In-Direct Customer)
**Content Requirements**
The system shall verify that:
- Organization Name is filled in.
- NTN is provided where applicable, or explicitly marked `N/A` if not applicable.
- Country of incorporation is stated.
- Proprietor / Directors / Partners details (including names and CNICs) are completely filled. This is mandatory.
- The appropriate checklist category is selected for "Nature of Business", "Organization Type", and "Business Classification". If none apply, `Others` must be selected and specified.
- License issue/details are mentioned where required.
- Required application information is complete and no fields are incorrectly left blank.
- **Account Match:** The Title of Account and Bank Account Number must exactly match what is written on the Account Maintenance Certificate.

**Signature & Stamp Requirements**
- **Every single page must be signed and stamped.**
- The form must contain the required applicant/authorized signatures according to the approved form.
- The form must contain the required official stamp.

---

## 5. Declaration of Sub-Biller
**Content Requirements**
The system shall verify that the declaration:
- Identifies the organization.
- Clearly states the intended sub-biller relationship.
- Contains the required organization information.
- Clearly indicates where applicable that **KPITB intends to act as a sub-biller / aggregator**.
- Contains the required declaration information.

**Signature & Stamp Requirements**
- The "Declaration of Sub-Member" section must be signed and stamped.
- The "Aggregator" section (belonging to KPITB) must also be properly signed and stamped.
- The system shall verify the required signature presence, official stamp presence, and information associated with them.

---

## 6. Tripartite Agreement
**Content Requirements**
The system shall verify that:
- All three participating organizations are named: 1-Link (Biller), KPITB (Bill Aggregator), and the Sub-biller (the onboarding department).
- Organization names are complete, consistent, and correctly written everywhere. **(Watch out for placeholder copy-paste errors like "Government Degree College" when it should be another department).**
- Required agreement sections are present.
- Point numbering is correct and sequential (All bullet/number points are complete, detailing documents present).
- Required text and account information are present.
- The agreement follows the prescribed structure and is checked in detail rather than only checking its existence.
- **Bank Details Verification:** Under the bank details section (e.g., bullet point 2, sub-point 5), the Account Title and Branch must exactly match the Account Maintenance Certificate.

**Date Requirement**
- The agreement **should not contain an entered date** in the first 2 lines (preamble). Dates must remain blank where the approved template requires it. An incorrectly populated date shall be flagged.

**Signature & Stamp Requirements**
- Must contain the required signatures of 1-Link, the Sub-biller, the Bill Aggregator, and the respective witnesses for each party.
- Must contain the required official stamp(s).
- The system shall verify stamp presence, location, readability, and required stamp information.

**Witness Requirement**
- Required witnesses are present for all parties.
- Witness information and signatures are provided.

---

## 7. Bilateral Agreement (SLA)
**Content Requirements**
The system shall verify:
- Required organization names (specific department must be clearly stated).
- Complete organization names.
- Required sections and correct point numbering.
- Required text (The main body text A, B, C, D points must remain intact and unmodified from the standard template).
- Required agreement structure and prescribed text formatting where formatting is a business requirement.

**Platform Terminology (PayMin / Digital Muhasil / Paymere BCX)**
- The document must explicitly mention the correct digital platform, e.g., "Digital Muhasil" or "PayMin" / "Paymere BCX". 
- The complete wording must be present, and **parallel line-to-line formatting must be maintained** exactly as the standard template.

**Section 5 — Transaction Charges**
- Transaction charges must be explicitly mentioned in Section 5.2 (e.g., "as per prevailing charges of 1-Link").
- Charges must be stated in PKR.
- Required transaction-charge information is complete.

**Section 6 — Account Information**
- The relevant bank account numbers must be listed.
- The account number here must exactly match the Account Maintenance Certificate and Tripartite Agreement.

**Date Requirement**
- The effective date and authorized person details should be checked, but dates that are required to remain blank **must remain blank**.

**Signature & Stamp Requirements**
- Must be signed and stamped everywhere required.
- Ensure Party A and Party B signatures and witnesses are present and consistent. If Party B witnesses are missing, verification is required if another director signed as a witness.

---

## 8. E-Stamp Papers & Notary Public (Participation Memorandum & SLA)
**Content Requirements**
- **Visual Authentication:** The print should visually resemble an original E-stamp paper (typically a brownish color texture), not just a plain A4 white paper printout.
- **Text Validation:** Check the Non-judicial description, First Party, and Second Party details on the stamp paper to ensure they are accurate and match the intended agreement.
- **Watermark:** Ensure the E-stamp watermark and extensions are valid. E-stamps shall be accepted as valid evidence and may be cross-checked against official online information.

**Notary Public Requirements**
- Where notarization is required, the document must contain a **Notary Public stamp/seal**.
- Required notarial information must be present.
- The notary public stamp and the picture/signature of the notary guy must be clearly visible and verifiable.

---

## 9. Schedule of Charges Agreement
**Content Requirements**
The system shall verify:
- Required charge and transaction information.
- Correct currency where applicable.
- Required document structure.
- Complete and readable information.

**Signature & Stamp Requirements**
- Must contain the required signature(s) and official stamp.
- The system shall verify stamp presence and readability.

---

## 10. Business Requirement Document (BRD)
**Content Requirements**
The system shall verify that the BRD:
- Identifies and lists all the revenue-generating services the department provides (e.g., filming fees, photography fees, ticket entries) that the organization wants to digitize.
- Confirms that these payments are required to be digitized.
- Does not omit required services.
- Follows the required structure and properly documents requested services.

**Signature & Stamp Requirements**
- Must contain the required signature where prescribed.
- Must contain an official stamp where required by its approved format.

---

## 11. Formal Request Letter
**Content Requirements**
The system shall verify that:
- The formal request is present and explicitly requests that KPITB onboard them as a sub-biller.
- **Subject Line:** The subject must clearly state the intent, for example: *"Onboarding as a sub-biller with KPITB"*.
- Required organization information, request details, and readable information are present.

**Signature & Stamp Requirements**
- Must contain the required authorized signature and official stamp where prescribed.

---

## 12. CNIC Copies Verification
The system shall verify:
- CNIC copies for all authorized persons (Proprietor, Directors, Partners) are present and attached.
- CNIC follows the required format and is not expired.
- CNIC is clearly readable.
- CNIC information is consistent with the relevant applicant/proprietor information.

---

## 13. Cross-Document Consistency (Critical Checks)
**Account Consistency**
The exact same bank account information (Account Title, Account Number, Branch Number/Name, IBAN) must be perfectly consistent across:
1. Account Maintenance Certificate
2. Bilateral Agreement
3. Tripartite Agreement
*Note: A mismatch shall generate a business-rule failure or human-review flag.*

**Organization Consistency**
The system shall compare organization names across all relevant documents to identify:
- Missing organization names
- Different organization names
- Inconsistent spelling or unexpected abbreviations
- Incomplete names

---

## 14. Document Formatting and Structure
Where an approved template exists, the system shall verify:
- Required sections, points, correct point numbering, wording, and fields.
- Required text formatting where explicitly part of the requirement.
- Correct placement of information.
- Dates populated only where required, and dates left blank where the template requires them to remain blank.
- Validation is based on the approved document templates, not generic assumptions.

---

## 15. Human Verification & Verification Principle
Automated verification does not constitute the final decision. The system checks signature presence and required location, not signature authenticity. The authorized employee remains the final decision-maker.

**The employee shall review (Manual Review Required):**
- Low-confidence OCR fields.
- Uncertain signatures or stamps.
- Account/IBAN mismatches.
- Organization-name discrepancies.
- Formatting discrepancies or Agreement inconsistencies.
- CNIC discrepancies.
- Possible tampering or originality concerns.

**Rule Result Classification:**
- **PASS:** The requirement is satisfied with sufficient confidence.
- **WARNING:** A potential issue exists but does not automatically block processing.
- **MANUAL REVIEW REQUIRED:** The system cannot reliably determine whether the requirement is satisfied.
- **FAIL:** A required business rule is clearly violated.
- **REJECTED:** The authorized employee has made the final rejection decision.

**Verification Principle:**
The system shall not consider OCR text alone sufficient evidence that a financial document is valid. Verification must combine OCR, Regex/Field Extraction, Cross-Document Comparison, Signature & Stamp Detection, and Business Rules.
